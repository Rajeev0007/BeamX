# BeamX Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

BeamX is made by **Rajeev Development**. In short: **BeamX doesn't collect
your data.** There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your device
- Your settings, device ID, trusted devices and profiles.
- Your access password is **never stored**. Only a derived key that can't
  be turned back into the password is kept.
- A diagnostics log of connection events, kept in memory only and deleted
  when BeamX closes. It contains no passwords, codes or keys, and is only
  shared if you copy and send it yourself.

## What goes between your devices
Screen, sound, microphone, files, clipboard text and remote-control input
travel **directly between your devices**, end-to-end encrypted. We never
receive them.

## What other services can see
- **Same network:** no outside server is contacted.
- **Connect from anywhere (public server):** the PeerJS public server
  (0.peerjs.com, run by the PeerJS project) sees your device's 9-digit ID,
  your IP address and encrypted setup messages. Public STUN servers
  (Google, Cloudflare) see your IP address to help find a direct route.
  None of them can see your screen, files, password or what you type.
- **Your own server:** if you set one, it replaces the public server and
  sees the same kind of information.
- **Downloads and updates:** GitHub hosts the releases, under GitHub's own
  privacy policy.

## Permissions BeamX asks for
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Screen recording / casting | To share your screen, only when you start sharing |
| Microphone | Only if you turn on mic sharing |
| Camera | Only to scan pairing QR codes |
| Local network | To find and connect to your devices |
| Notifications | To show that your screen is being shared |
| Accessibility (BeamX Control edition only) | To let a device you allowed tap and type on this phone. It reads no screen content. |

## Children
BeamX isn't directed at children under 13.
